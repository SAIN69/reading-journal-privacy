# Privacy Policy for R.J. – Your Personal Journal

**Effective date: October 10, 2026**

R.J. – Your Personal Journal is developed by **SAIN (Б.К)**.

## Information stored by the app

R.J. – Your Personal Journal stores your journal information locally on your device.

This may include:

- books and authors
- reading status and progress
- ratings
- notes and quotes
- reading streak data
- application language and appearance preferences
- reading streak reminder preferences

This information is stored locally on your device and is not sent to the developer.

## Data collection

R.J. – Your Personal Journal does not require a user account.

The application does not currently use:

- Firebase
- analytics services
- advertising services
- cloud synchronization
- developer-operated servers for storing journal data
- tracking services

Your journal data remains on your device unless you explicitly choose to export or share it.

## Notifications and reading streak reminders

R.J. – Your Personal Journal may request permission to send notifications.

Notification permission is used only to provide optional reading streak reminders, such as reminding you when your current reading streak is about to expire.

Reading streak reminders are generated locally on your device.

Your reading activity, streak information, notification content, and reminder preferences are not sent to the developer or to an external server.

You can enable or disable reading streak reminders from the application settings.

You can also control notification permission through your Android device settings.

Notifications are not used for advertising, analytics, tracking, or marketing purposes.

## Sharing and exporting notes

When you choose to export or share notes, R.J. – Your Personal Journal uses Android's standard sharing functionality.

You decide which application or service receives the information.

Information is only shared when you explicitly choose to use the sharing feature.

## Data retention and deletion

Your data remains stored locally on your device until you delete it.

You can remove data by:

- deleting individual books or notes
- using **Delete All Local Data**
- clearing the application's data through Android settings
- uninstalling the application

The **Delete All Local Data** option removes locally stored books, notes, reading progress, streak information, and resets application preferences.

## Backups

R.J. – Your Personal Journal does not provide cloud backup or cloud synchronization.

The application is designed to keep journal data locally on your device.

## Advertising and analytics

R.J. – Your Personal Journal does not currently use advertising or analytics services.

The application does not use your journal information for advertising, profiling, or tracking.

## Data sharing

R.J. – Your Personal Journal does not sell your personal information.

The developer does not receive or share your locally stored journal information.

Data may leave your device only when you explicitly choose to export or share information using Android's sharing functionality.

## Security

R.J. – Your Personal Journal relies on Android's application storage and security mechanisms to protect locally stored application data.

No method of electronic storage can guarantee absolute security, but the application is designed to minimize data exposure by keeping journal information locally on your device.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application changes or when new features are introduced.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
