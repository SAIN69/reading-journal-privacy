# Privacy Policy for R.J. – Your Personal Journal

**Effective date: September 27, 2026**

R.J. – Your Personal Journal is developed by **SAIN (Б.К)**.

## Information stored by the app

R.J. – Your Personal Journal stores books, notes, reading progress, streak data, and application preferences locally on your device.

## Data collection

The application does not require a user account and does not use Firebase, analytics, advertising services, cloud synchronization, or developer-operated servers for storing journal data.

Your journal data remains on your device unless you explicitly choose to export or share it.

## Sharing and exporting notes

When you choose to share notes, the app uses Android's standard sharing functionality.

You decide which application or service receives the information.

## Data retention and deletion

Your data remains stored locally until you delete it.

You can remove data by:
- deleting individual books or notes
- using **Delete All Local Data**
- clearing the application's data in Android settings
- uninstalling the application

The **Delete All Local Data** option removes books, notes, reading progress, streak information, and resets application preferences.

## Backups

R.J. – Your Personal Journal does not provide cloud backup or cloud synchronization.

## Advertising and analytics

R.J. – Your Personal Journal does not currently use advertising or analytics services.
